Read `.claude/standards/dev-standards.md` first (owner's goals, his taste: melodic house/techno, Ben Böhmer, Stephan Bodzin, Afterlife). Read `.claude/agents/music-researcher.md` and follow it as your role.

## The two items (both size L, designed this check-in in parallel)
- **AI-1 · Song sections by voice** (assistant-dev): the user says "make an 8-bar intro, then build up and drop" and the assistant arranges the song into sections that can be changed by talking ("make the break longer", "drop the drums in the outro", "add a second drop"). Done when: a full arrangement (intro, build, drop, break, outro) can be made and changed by voice.
- **UX-1 · Arrangement view like Ableton** (studio-ux-dev): a longer timeline where sections and clips are placed across a whole song, instead of one repeating loop. Done when: a 3-minute song can be laid out, edited and played through.

## Questions
1. How do Ableton (Arrangement view, Session-to-Arrangement, locators), FL Studio (Playlist + patterns), Logic (arrangement markers / Arrangement track, Session Players) and Bitwig represent song structure? What is the smallest model that feels like Ableton to a producer: clips on a timeline per track, sections/markers, loop brace?
2. Which interactions make an arrangement view feel good on desktop and tablet: drag/resize/duplicate clips, Cmd+D, loop region, zoom, section markers, scrolling playhead, muting a clip?
3. AI tools that arrange by prompt (Suno, Udio, Ableton/Logic AI features, Band-in-a-Box, Mixed In Key Captain, Landr, Splice, any 2025-2026 "talk to a DAW" tools): how do they let users describe or change song structure? What works, what frustrates users?
4. Melodic house/techno arrangement conventions (Böhmer, Bodzin, Afterlife): typical section lengths in bars, where elements enter and leave (kick out in break, filter builds, risers, snare rolls), typical track length at 120-126 bpm. Give a default template the assistant could use.
5. What would delight a producer here, and what are the risks (complexity, phone/tablet, performance)?
