# RES-5 research brief (music-researcher), 13 Nov 2026 14:25 IST

Read `.claude/standards/dev-standards.md` first (copyright rule; everything stays in the user's browser; no third-party services without the owner's approval).

The owner's request (HQ, 13 Nov 13:03 IST, his words): "I would essentially want the keys to have the feature to extract individual elements like drums - kicks and hi-hats, melody, bass, instrument, vocal etc. and then the user can just ask keys to just play the background while they sing along like karaoke or producers can create remixes by just extracting some elements and then add on their own or ask you to by telling them their vision".

Studio Keys is one static web page (plain JS, about 9.5 MB) plus a Cloudflare Worker (Workers AI, Llama 3.3). Songs and recordings stay in the browser. It already imports audio and turns audio into notes.

Research pack, focused and practical:
1. How others do stem separation and what users expect: Ableton Live 12 (stem separation), FL Studio (stem separation), Logic Pro (Stem Splitter), Moises, LALAL.AI, Fadr, Serato/djay/rekordbox (real-time stems), karaoke apps. Which stems they offer (vocals, drums, bass, other/piano/guitar; kick/snare/hats sub-splits), quality, speed, price.
2. Can it run IN THE BROWSER on a laptop and a phone? Open models (Demucs v4 / HTDemucs and its licence, Spleeter, Open-Unmix, MDX/BS-RoFormer/Mel-RoFormer, any 2025-2026 small models), ONNX Runtime Web / WebGPU / WASM ports that exist, model download size, time to split a 3-minute song on a typical laptop and a phone, memory. Drum sub-splitting (kick/snare/hats) options.
3. Server options if the browser is too slow: e.g. a model on Cloudflare Workers AI (does one exist?), or a paid API (which, cost per song). Flag privacy (songs leave the browser) and cost: both need the owner's approval.
4. Copyright and rights: splitting songs the user owns vs. commercial tracks; how Moises/others word it; what we should say in the app.
5. Recommendation: the best first version for Studio Keys (e.g. "4 stems in-browser on laptop, 'play the background' karaoke mode, remix by muting stems and adding parts"), what to add later, and what it would take.
End with "For the devs" notes (bullets for sound-engine-dev, studio-ux-dev, assistant-dev) and 2-3 ideas in the HQ research format.

Second, short question (one paragraph each) for the UX-4 phone design: (a) how comparable music apps (BandLab, Koala Sampler, GarageBand, FL Studio Mobile, Suno, Moises) lay out their phone screens and whether any put an AI/voice assistant first; (b) PWA vs Capacitor vs TWA for a web music app in 2026: microphone, Web Audio latency and Web MIDI support on iPhone and Android, and store rules for web-wrapper apps.
