## The bugs (tester's daily session, 17 Nov; full report `.claude/work/reports/QA-1-daily-2026-11-17.md`; scripts and files in /tmp/qa/2026-11-17/)
1. **MEDIUM — The pitch wheel and mod wheel bend the whole song, drums included.** Make a beat with a bassline, press Play, and without pressing a key push the pitch wheel up: the song's bass goes 110.0 → 123.5 Hz (+200 cents). Mod wheel at 127 makes the song's bass wobble 108.4–112.8 Hz. All 152 drum voices played in 2 s were wired to the wheels.
   - Tester's read (check it): `Voice.src()` in `src/core.js` connects every voice in the live AudioContext to `A.bend` and `A.vibGain`, including the song's scheduled notes.
   - Should: the wheels change only the notes played live on the keys (MIDI keyboard, on-screen keys, computer keys) — like any DAW where the wheel belongs to the instrument you're playing. Song notes, drums and recorded/generated parts never move. Keep the live-played feel exactly as now (bend range, vibrato depth and rate). Think about the corner cases: a note held while recording; the looper; notes played while the song isn't running; offline renders/exports must never pick up the wheels.
2. **LOW — The Sound Lab makes an instrument out of silence.** Sound Lab: load a silent file → Extract → Add as sampler: you get "Lead synth from 'silence'" (no root found, noise 1.00), and Add puts it on the rack and the keys, which then play nothing.
   - Should: when the chosen part is silent (or nearly — pick a sensible threshold, e.g. peak below about -50 dBFS), say "That part is silent — pick a section with sound" and keep Add disabled. A quiet but real sound must still work.
