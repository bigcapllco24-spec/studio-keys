## The bugs (tester's daily session, 17 Nov; full report `.claude/work/reports/QA-1-daily-2026-11-17.md`; scripts, test files and screenshots in /tmp/qa/2026-11-17/ — the 16-bar MIDI is /tmp/qa/2026-11-17/fx/long16.mid)
1. **MEDIUM — Importing a MIDI file longer than the song silently drops most of it.** New song → "drums like Ben Böhmer" (4 bars) → import a 16-bar MIDI (melody 64 notes, drums 96), by picker or drag-in. The toast says "Imported 2 MIDI tracks", but Melody has 16 of 64 notes and Drums 24 of 96; the song stays 4 bars, and adding bars later doesn't bring them back.
   - Tester's read: `importFile` in `src/edit.js` drops notes past the song's end (`e.b<L`).
   - Decided (no owner choice needed — it's how MIDI into an empty song already works): **keep every note and grow the loop/song to fit the file** (round up to whole bars, respecting any max the app has), toast e.g. "Imported 2 MIDI tracks · the loop is now 16 bars", one Undo puts it all back. Existing parts keep playing as before (they repeat or stay as they are — say which and why). If the file is longer than the app's maximum, keep what fits and say so plainly.
2. **MEDIUM — Song view: moving or shortening a looped section while it plays makes playback leave the loop and stop.** (A) Full track, double-click Drop 2 to loop it, drag Drop 2 to the start: the playhead carries on at bar 96 in "Drop 3", plays to the end and stops; the label still says Drop 2. (B) Loop an 8-bar Drop; in its second half choose Shorter (or drag its edge to 4 bars): it plays the Outro and stops.
   - Tester's read: `songTick` in `src/song.js` only wraps when it crosses the loop end from inside (`b0<lr[1]&&b1>lr[1]`).
   - Should: the loop follows its section (moved, resized, duplicated-from), and if the playhead ends up outside the loop range it jumps back to the loop start on the next tick (no stuck notes, no double triggers). Deleting the looped section: clear the loop and keep playing as now.
3. **LOW — Both MP3 downloads start 25 ms late.** Decoded against the app's own render, the loop MP3 and the song MP3 are 1105 samples (25.1 ms) late; a 4-bar loop at 124 BPM is 7.784 s instead of 7.742 s. lamejs adds encoder delay and the file carries no gapless info.
   - Should: the decoded MP3 starts on the beat and the 1-loop MP3 is the loop's length within a few ms in Chrome's decoder (and ideally in DAWs). Options: write a Xing/LAME info frame with the encoder delay and padding (players that honour gapless trim it), and/or compensate in the encode. Pick what actually measures right in Chrome `decodeAudioData`, and say what you chose. Keep WAV untouched.
4. **Small rough edges, same files (do them if quick):** the corrupt-MIDI toast shows a raw JS error ("Offset is outside the bounds of the DataView") — make it "That MIDI file couldn't be read"; "Only the first 1 bars play" → "bar".
