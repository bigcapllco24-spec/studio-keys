## The bug (found by qa-tester in QA-2 fix 1, `.claude/work/reports/QA-2-fix1.md`, "Found on the way" 1; repro `.claude/work/reports/qa2-probes/dbl.mjs`)
In Song view, a slightly slow double-click on a song part is lost: the part switches off and the editor stays closed. `src/song.js` ~458-459: the `.part` click handler starts a 230 ms timer to toggle the part; if the second press comes before the timer fires but is released after it, the toggle fires and the dblclick never opens the editor. On an idle machine these gaps (release 1 → press 2 / press 2 → release 2) lost it: 150/150, 200/100, 100/200 ms; 80/0 and 300/50 worked. A normal human double-click can hit this, a tablet double-tap more often.
Fix idea: cancel the pending toggle on the second pointerdown/mousedown on the same part (e.detail>1 or a press within the OS double-click window), and decide on dblclick; or undo a toggle that fired inside the double-click (untogglePart already exists, check it restores cleanly with no leftover undo step). Single click must still switch the part after the same delay; a double-tap on touch opens the editor. One user action = one undo step; a lost double-click must not leave a toggle in undo history.
