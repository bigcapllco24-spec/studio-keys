# UX-11: Mute and solo single drum sounds (kick, clap, hats, open hats…)

Owner's request (15 Nov): "When Keys generates an element like drums, you can't mute the individual elements from it, like open hats etc. Enable that."

## 1. What and why
A Keys beat is one drums track, so today you can only mute the whole beat. With this change every sound inside a beat (kick, clap, snare, closed hat, open hat, shaker, perc, toms, crash, ride) gets its own **M** (mute) and **S** (solo) button, in the drum editor and in a small **Kit** panel on the track. You can also just say it: "Keys, mute the open hats". Muting keeps the notes, so you can bring a sound back at any time. What you hear is what you export.

## 2. User flow

**A. By voice (main path)**
1. A Ben Böhmer beat is playing. The user says "Keys, mute the open hats."
2. Within about 0.1 s the open hats stop. The rest of the beat keeps playing. Keys says: "Open hats muted. Say 'bring them back' when you want them."
3. The drums track's new **Kit** button now shows **KIT 1** in the accent colour, so it's clear that one sound is off.
4. "Solo the kick": only the kick plays. Keys says: "Just the kick now. Say 'hear all the drums' to bring everything back."
5. "Bring the claps back": the claps play again. "Unmute everything": every drum sound plays again, and any muted tracks are unmuted too.
6. "Undo" (or Ctrl+Z) undoes the last mute or solo, like every other change.

**B. In the drum editor (Edit notes on a drums track)**
1. Each row label (Crash … Kick) now has two small buttons, **M** and **S**.
2. Click **M** on Open hat: the row's hits fade (the notes stay where they are) and the open hats go silent. A chip appears next to Draw/Select: **"1 sound muted · Unmute all"**.
3. Click **S** on Kick: only the kick plays and every other row fades. The chip reads **"Solo: Kick · Hear all"**. Ctrl/Cmd+click on another **S** adds that sound to the solo (Ableton-style). Clicking a lit **S** again turns the solo off.
4. Rows with no hits (Cowbell, toms…) show their buttons dimmed but still clickable.
5. On a phone or tablet, the row labels with M/S stay in view while the steps scroll sideways. Today the labels scroll away.
6. The editor hint gains: "M mutes a sound, S plays only that sound (Ctrl/Cmd+S adds more)".

**C. From the track list (no editor needed)**
1. Drum tracks get a **KIT** button between FX and M. Other tracks don't get one.
2. Clicking it opens a panel under the track, the same way FX does: **"Sounds in Drums"**, with one chip for each sound that has hits (name, number of hits, M, S), plus "Unmute all", "Edit notes", and the hint "Or say 'Keys, mute the open hats'".
3. A muted chip shows the sound's name crossed out. In the track's clip preview, muted hits are drawn faded.
4. When the panel is closed, **KIT n** stays lit in the accent outline while n sounds are muted or soloed. Nothing is hidden.

## 4. How it will be built

### Data (per track, saved, undoable)
- `t.muteNotes`: array of drum MIDI pitches that are muted, e.g. `[46]`.
- `t.soloNotes`: array of pitches that are soloed, e.g. `[36]`.
- Both are left out when empty, so old songs load unchanged. They are plain JSON on the track, so they save to IndexedDB, travel in `song.json`, copy on Duplicate (the copy sounds the same as the original) and are captured by the existing undo snapshots in `saveProject`.
- They are stored by **pitch** (one per editor row), because that is what the user sees. Words like "hats" or "perc" are turned into pitches when the command runs (see the voice section).
- One rule decides whether a hit is heard: `drumRowOff(t,n)` = the track is a drum track AND (`n` is in `muteNotes`, OR `soloNotes` is not empty and `n` is not in it). Non-drum tracks are never affected, so if a drums track is switched to a piano, nothing is silently missing.

### Playback: all in `src/studio.js` (mine), no sound-engine file
- `scheduleEvent(tr,e,t,dur)`: return early when `drumRowOff(tr,e.n)`, before the kick sidechain duck. Live loop, overdub and Song-mode playback (`songSchedule` → `scheduleEvent`) all pass through here. A muted kick doesn't pump the bass either.
- `renderAudio` (loop MP3, WAV mix and stems in the zip): skip off rows in the kick-duck list and in the note loop.
- `renderSongSpan` (Song-mode MP3): the same two skips.
- MIDI export (`exportMidiOnly`, zip MIDI): leave out off rows, matching how a muted track is left out today.
- `trackAudible` in `src/mixer.js` is **not** touched. `songFlat` (`src/song.js`) is not touched either, so its cache keeps working: section "without" drops (e.g. the intro without clap) and track-level mutes simply combine.
- Toggling while playing takes effect at the next scheduled hit (within the 120 ms look-ahead). A hit that is already ringing finishes its tail.

### UI: `src/daw.js`, `src/head.html` (mine)
- Drum editor: an HTML row header over the canvas label column (`edGeom` K grows from 78 to about 150 px). Each row has a label (click still previews the sound), then M and S buttons with `aria-pressed`, `aria-label="Mute Open hat"` and keyboard focus. Its row heights come from the same `edGeom()`. It follows horizontal scroll, so labels stay in view on phones.
- `drawEditor`: fade off rows and their velocity bars. Header chip "n sounds muted · Unmute all" or "Solo: … · Hear all". Updated `edHint`.
- Track list: a `KIT` `.ib.fxb` button on drum tracks only, with a count of muted plus soloed sounds. A `KITOPEN` set mirrors `FXOPEN`. The panel lists rows that have hits, plus any muted row without hits, so a mute is never invisible.
- `drawRolls`: fade off rows in the clip preview.
- Every toggle goes through `undoBoundary(); saveProject(); renderTracks()`, so it is one undo step.
- Shared helpers in `src/studio.js` that both the UI and `actions.js` use: `drumRowOff(t,n)`, `drumSoundPitches(t,word)` (word → pitches: uses `DRUM_GROUPS` from `song.js`, `DRUM_WORD` from `actions.js`, and `drumPiece` over the track's pitches plus `DRUM_ROWS`), and `setDrumSounds(t,op,pitches,{add})`, which returns a short description like "Open hat muted".

### Voice: assistant-dev builds this part (Worker prompt + `actions.js` + `maker.js` + `assistant.js` context)
New action, exact spec:

```
{"type":"drum_sounds","track":"drums","sounds":["open hat"],"do":"mute"}
```
- `do`:
  - `mute`: add these sounds to the muted ones.
  - `unmute`: "bring the claps back".
  - `solo`: play only these sounds, replacing any solo. With `"add":true` they are added to the solo.
  - `unsolo`.
  - `reset`: clear all mutes and solos; `sounds` is not needed.
- `sounds`: words. `kick`, `snare`, `clap`, `rim`, `hats` (all hats), `closed hat`, `open hat`, `pedal hat`, `shaker` (= pedal hat 44, which the grooves use for shakers), `perc` (rim, cowbell, toms, as in `DRUM_GROUPS.perc`), `toms`, `cowbell`, `crash`, `ride`. Row labels work too.
- `track`: optional. If it's missing: the drum track open in the editor, else the selected drum track, else the last drums track. For `reset` with no track: every drum track.
- Executor (`actions.js`): `drum_sounds(a)` → `setDrumSounds(...)`. It returns e.g. "Open hat muted on Drums". If a word matches no pitch, it returns nothing, so Keys says it couldn't find that sound.
- `assistant.js` song context: add `mutedSounds:["Open hat"]` and `soloSounds:[…]` to each drum track, so the AI knows the state and can remind the user.
- **Worker prompt** text to add under ACTIONS:
  `- {"type":"drum_sounds","track":"drums","sounds":["open hat"],"do":"mute"}: mutes or solos single sounds INSIDE a drums track; the notes stay. do: mute, unmute, solo (only these play; "add":true adds to the solo), unsolo, reset (everything plays again). sounds: kick, snare, clap, rim, hats, closed hat, open hat, shaker, perc, toms, cowbell, crash, ride. Use it for "mute the open hats", "solo the kick", "bring the claps back", "just kick and hats", "kill the shaker for now".`
  Under RULES: `- "Mute / solo / bring back <a drum sound>" is drum_sounds, never mix (that mutes the whole track) and never groove (that rewrites the beat). Only "remove", "delete" or "no more <sound> in the beat" use groove. "Unmute everything" = drum_sounds reset plus mix mute:false for each muted track. After vary on drums, if the context shows mutedSounds, say they are still muted.`
  Example: `User: "mute the open hats" → {"reply":"Open hats muted. Say 'bring them back' when you want them.","actions":[{"type":"drum_sounds","sounds":["open hat"],"do":"mute"}]}`
- **maker.js** (offline): before the `mixVerb && role` branch (today "mute the hats" mutes the whole drums track), match `(mute|unmute|solo|bring back|turn off|kill|silence)` together with a drum-sound word → `drum_sounds`. Handle "unmute/hear everything|all (the drums)" → `reset`, and "just (the) kick and hats" → `solo`. "Mute the drums" still mutes the whole track.
- Smoke-test lines (qa-tester, or I add them if the brief allows):
  1. `localBrain('mute the open hats')` → drums `muteNotes` = [46], track not muted.
  2. `'solo the kick'` → `soloNotes` = [36]; `renderAudio` of the drums has peak > 0.05, and the rendered kick-only loop matches a kick-only copy within 1 dB.
  3. `'unmute everything'` → both cleared.
  4. `undo()` restores the previous state.
  5. Editor M button click → `aria-pressed="true"`, chip visible.

## 5. Edge cases
- **"Another one" / vary / groove changes:** the track object is kept (`addTrack` with `replaceRole` reuses it), so mutes **stay**. This matches Ableton, where pad mutes live on the instrument, not the clip. Keys says "Open hats are still muted". A muted sound that has no hits in the new groove keeps its mute; it shows as a lit M on an empty row and stays listed in the Kit panel, so it's never a hidden trap. (Decision 1.)
- **New drums track** (add_drums when no generated drums exist, or "+ Empty beat"): starts with nothing muted.
- **A beat made by the user** ("+ Empty beat", or any notes track on a drum kit): works the same, because it is keyed on `isDrumTrack`.
- **Duplicate track:** copies its mutes and solos, so the copy sounds identical.
- **Song mode, patterns A/B:** mutes belong to the track, so they apply in every section and to every pattern. A section's own "without clap" still works on top. Both must allow a hit for it to play.
- **Whole-track M/S:** unchanged and independent. Track solo picks which tracks play; sound solo picks which sounds play inside the drums.
- **Singing a part (only drums play):** muted drum sounds stay muted. This is useful, for example to sing over just the kick.
- **Playing pads or keys live:** your own hits always sound, even on a muted row. Mute/solo affects playback of the part only. Hits recorded onto a muted row are kept but stay silent, and a toast says "Open hat is muted on this track".
- **Instrument switched from a kit to a melodic sound:** the filter only applies to drum tracks, so nothing goes missing. The arrays are kept in case the user switches back.
- **Everything muted:** the beat is silent, but the track isn't marked muted. The KIT badge and the editor chip show why.
- **MP3/WAV/MIDI export:** muted sounds are left out, matching what you hear. That's the same rule as a muted track today.

## 6. Effort, risks, decisions

**Effort: M overall.** studio-ux-dev M (row header, Kit panel, fades, playback and export filters, phone and theme checks). assistant-dev S (one action in three places plus context). No sound-engine work.

**Risks**
- A user forgets a muted sound and thinks the open hats "vanished". Mitigation: KIT n badge, editor chip, crossed-out chip, Keys mentions it after "another one", and "unmute everything" always works.
- The AI may confuse three meanings: mute a sound, rewrite the beat without it, or mute the whole track. Mitigation: explicit prompt rule and example, maker.js ordering, and a Llama check with 5-6 phrasings before release (lead).
- The HTML row header must stay lined up with the canvas rows at every zoom, phone width and theme. It takes its geometry from `edGeom()` and gets a test at 1400, 390 and 360 px.
- On phones the header uses about 150 px, so about one bar of steps shows at a time. The grid already scrolls today; the labels now stay put.
- Performance: one tiny array check per scheduled hit. Negligible.

**Decisions for the owner (max 3)**
1. **"Another one" keeps the mutes?**
   - Options: (a) keep them, and Keys says so; (b) clear them on every new groove.
   - **Recommend (a):** you muted the open hats because you don't want them. Ableton behaves the same way.
2. **Quick Kit panel in the track list as well as in the editor?**
   - Options: (a) yes, a KIT button on drum tracks only; (b) editor only.
   - **Recommend (a):** one tap from the track, and the KIT n badge shows at a glance that something is muted.
3. **What does "take out / drop / no more open hats" mean?**
   - Options: (a) mute (reversible, notes kept); only "remove" or "delete" rewrites the beat; (b) always rewrite the beat without them.
   - **Recommend (a):** safer, and undo isn't even needed to get them back.
