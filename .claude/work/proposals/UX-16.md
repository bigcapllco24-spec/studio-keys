# UX-16 · An Ableton-like studio, one element at a time · Design proposal
studio-ux-dev · 22 Nov 2026 · Design mode (no code changed; repo clean)
Research used: RES-7 part A (all 6 ranked elements, kept in the same order) and its "For the devs (UX-16)" notes. One addition: the computer keyboard plays notes (A W S E D F …, Z/X, 1-0), so every new shortcut uses Ctrl/Cmd+Alt and never a single letter (Bitwig's I/E/D/M keys would play notes).

## 1. What and why
On a laptop, Studio Keys is a long page today. The first screen is the controller. The tracks start about 1,700 px down, below the Keys box and two rows of transport. Sounds, Effects and Sing are further down still. Ableton is one fixed screen with the tracks in the middle, the transport on top, the detail at the bottom and nothing to scroll. We get there in six small steps, one per release. The first step is the frame: everything on one screen, the tracks in the centre, Keys on the right, and the controller in a drawer at the bottom.

## 2. The plan, ranked (one element per release)
| # | Step | What the user notices | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **One-screen studio** (≥ 1024 px wide) | No page scroll. Transport on top, tracks in the middle, Keys on the right, the controller in a drawer at the bottom, and Sounds/Effects/Sing/Lab/Controller in a side panel. The note editor docks at the bottom of the tracks. | **L** |
| 2 | Dense track lanes | Flat rows: 1 px lines instead of rounded cards. A 200 px header with name, M/S, FX and volume on one line, and 64 px lanes (24 px when folded). Colour only on clips. Twice as many tracks fit. | M |
| 3 | Control bar | The two top rows become one 40 px Live-style bar. Square buttons, drag the BPM to change it, a bars.beats.16ths counter, Space to play or stop anywhere, and the less-used options in a ⋯ menu. | S |
| 4 | Bottom Clip / Device panel | The docked editor gets a second tab, **Device**. It shows the selected track's instrument and each effect as a box with dials (FX moves out of the track row). Shift+Tab switches tabs. | M |
| 5 | Vertical mixer strips | The Mixer tab shows 76 px channel strips: tall fader, meter, pan, sends and M/S. Keys, Pads and Master strips sit on the right. | M |
| 6 | Browser sidebar and hint line | Sounds becomes a permanent, foldable 240 px list on the left that you can drag onto a track. A one-line hint at the bottom says what is under the mouse, shortcut included, like FL's hint bar. | M |

Steps 1-3 are what make it look like Ableton at first glance. Steps 4-6 make it fast to use. I recommend we ship them in this order. Step 1 comes first because the later steps all live inside the frame: step 2's lanes need the fixed centre, and steps 4 and 6 are the frame's bottom panel and left sidebar.

## 3. Step 1 in full: the one-screen studio
### Where everything goes (1440×900 shown; mockups b-f)
```
┌ App bar 40 px ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ▦ Song name  Saved 12:45  New Open Save [⬇ MP3] Export▾ │ Sounds Effects Sing Sound Lab Controller │ ●●● Sound on  MIDI │
├ Transport 40 px ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ● ▶ ■  [Playing loop]  2 . 3  Loop|Song  Make it a song▾ │ BPM 124 Tap Clock  A minor │ Length 4 bars ×2  Metronome  Count-in  Quantize  First loop │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────┤
│ Tracks | Mixer  Undo Redo            + Instrument + Beat …   │ (●) Keys   status  AI│
│ ruler                                                        │ chat (scrolls)       │
│ track cards (scroll inside this area only)                   │                      │
│                                                              │ chips: Another one … │
│ ── drag handle ──                                            │ 🎤 Sing the bassline │
│ Note editor, docked here when open (about 300 px, resizable) │ [Talk or type] Send  │
│                                                              │ Your sound · Settings│
├ Your controller ▾  Keyboard | Full controller | Hide   Keys Grand Piano  Pads …  Ctrl+Alt+K ┤
│ 61-key keyboard (about 112 px)  — or the full controller (wheels, LCD, 8 knobs, pads) │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
- **When it applies:** screens at least 1024 px wide and 600 px tall that aren't in the phone layout (`html[data-frame]`). Phones keep UX-4's tab bar. Tablets from 601 to 1023 px keep today's page. Nothing about phones changes.
- **The page never scrolls.** Only the track area, the Keys chat and the side panel scroll inside themselves.
- **App bar:** the song name (Fraunces, click to rename), save state, New / Open / Save, **⬇ MP3** and an **Export ▾** menu (MP3 length, Ableton .zip, MIDI only). The old top-nav links become **section buttons** that open the side panel. The theme dots, the sound pill and the MIDI pill stay; clicking the MIDI pill opens the Controller tab. The big brand header goes, and the logo mark stays.
- **Transport row:** today's two rows of controls in one compact row (26 px controls, 4 px corners). Every control and id is kept: Rec, Play, Stop, the loop button, position, Loop/Song, Make it a song, BPM, Tap, Clock, key, Length ×2, Metronome, Count-in, Quantize and First loop. On narrower screens First loop moves into a small ⋯ menu. Step 3 later turns this row into the polished Live bar.
- **Centre:** the view tabs row (Tracks | Mixer, Undo/Redo, the + buttons, Voice note, Sing a melody, Import), the ruler, then the tracks.
  - Step 1 keeps today's track cards unchanged. Step 2 makes them dense.
  - Song view works the same here, with its section strip on top.
  - Files can still be dropped on the tracks.
  - Mixer shows today's mixer plus the Keys / Pads / Master "Live mixer" cards, which used to sit under the tracks.
- **Docked editor:** "Edit notes" or a double-click opens today's piano roll in the bottom part of the centre column. It doesn't push the page down any more.
  - Drag its top edge to resize it (about 200 px to 60% of the height; the size is remembered). Esc or ✕ closes it.
  - Draw/Select (B), box select, Alt+drag copy, Ctrl/Cmd+C/V/D/A, arrows, the velocity lane and undo all stay the same. Only its container and height change.
- **Keys, right sidebar, 320 px:**
  - A 40 px **flat** orb with the status line and the AI/Basic dot. The orb loses its radial gradient and glow, to follow the matte rule.
  - The chat fills the height, and the newest message sits next to the input.
  - The chips include 🎤 Sing the bassline, Sing chords and Sing a melody.
  - The input field and Send are at the bottom.
  - "Your sound · Settings ▸" at the foot opens the Spotify/artists box and the listening options in place of the chat; ◂ Back returns to the chat.
  - A » button folds the sidebar to a 48 px rail that shows only the orb, so the orb still works.
- **Controller drawer, full width, bottom:** a 28 px header shows the keys sound, the pads sound, the octave and the sustain/mod lights, with three modes:
  - **Keyboard** (61 keys, about 112 px): every key you play lights up, and you can click or drag on it as today.
  - **Full controller:** today's wheels, LCD, 8 knobs, pads, hardware transport row and keyboard, the same look as today, about 390 px. It covers the lower part of the tracks while open.
  - **Hide:** the header row only.
  - Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+K shows or hides it. The choice is remembered (localStorage, try/catch). The speaker room picture behind the controller isn't shown in the frame, because it costs about 230 px. It stays on tablet and phone.
- **Side panel, left, over the tracks:** Sounds · Effects · Sing · Sound Lab · Controller as tabs. Each tab holds today's section, moved in as is, with its search, cards, KEYS/PADS buttons, dials, tuner, Lab and MIDI profile.
  - The panel is 420 px wide; Sound Lab gets 720 px because it needs room for the waveform.
  - Esc, ✕ or a click outside closes it, and focus goes back to the button that opened it.
  - Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+B opens Sounds, as in Live's browser.
  - The controller Learn buttons and the sound credits move into the Controller tab. The credits stay one click away, which the CC BY licences need.

### 3a. Flow for step 1 (what the user sees and does)
1. **Open the page on a laptop.** Everything is on one screen. The empty track area says "No tracks yet. Ask Keys …", Keys is on the right with "Say 'Keys' or press the orb", the keyboard is at the bottom, and the transport is on top. Nothing is below the fold.
2. **Talk to Keys.** "Keys, 124 bpm drums with hats and claps like Ben Böhmer." The reply appears in the right sidebar while the Drums track appears in the centre, so both are visible at once (today the tracks are below the Keys box).
3. **Sing a part.** Click "🎤 Sing the bassline" in the sidebar, or say it. The count-in shows in the transport position box, and the take lands as a new track in the centre. The sidebar status shows the singing state as today.
4. **Edit notes.** Double-click the Bassline clip or press ✎ Edit notes. The piano roll docks at the bottom of the centre, and the tracks above stay visible and scroll. Drag its top edge to make it taller. Esc closes it.
5. **Play your controller.** Play a MIDI keyboard or the computer keys: the drawer's keyboard lights up. Click "Full controller" for the knobs, pads, LCD and wheels. Ctrl+Alt+K hides it all when you want more room for tracks.
6. **Pick a sound.** Click **Sounds** in the app bar (or Ctrl+Alt+B). The panel slides over the left of the tracks. Pick KEYS or PADS on a sound, or drag a card onto a track, then press Esc.
7. **Effects / Sing to play / Sound Lab / MIDI setup:** click the matching app-bar button, which opens the same panel on that tab.
8. **Export.** Use ⬇ MP3 in the app bar, or Export ▾ for an Ableton .zip or MIDI only.

## 4. How step 1 will be built
- **New `src/frame.js`** (about 200 lines, in the same style as UX-4's `phone.js`):
  - `FR_MQ = matchMedia('(min-width:1024px) and (min-height:600px)')`. When it matches and the phone layout is off, it sets `html[data-frame]` and moves the existing nodes into frame slots with a comment marker. Turning it off puts them back.
  - No controls are rebuilt, so every id, listener, smoke-test hook and `runActions` path keeps working.
  - It also holds the drawer modes, the side panel open/close (focus return, Esc), the editor resize handle, the Keys rail, the Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+K / +B shortcuts and the remembered state (`store` with try/catch).
  - **The lead adds one line** to `tools/build.py` to bundle it after `phone.js`.
- **`src/body.html`:** empty slots for the app bar, transport row, Keys sidebar, drawer header and side panel shell, plus the Export ▾ menu markup. The existing sections stay where they are in the source.
- **`src/head.html`:** one `html[data-frame]` block at the end, using theme tokens only: the 100vh grid, `overflow:hidden` on the page, 4 px corners on frame controls, a flat orb, and no playhead glow inside the frame. The phone and tablet rules aren't touched.
- **`src/daw.js`:**
  - `openEditor` stops calling `scrollIntoView` in the frame.
  - `edFitH` reads the docked panel height.
  - The playhead adds `#playlist.scrollTop`, because the track area now scrolls.
  - A `ResizeObserver` on the track area and the editor redraws the rolls, ruler and editor when the drawer, editor or sidebar changes size (today they only redraw on window resize).
- **`src/song.js`:** the same observer calls `songRelayout`.
- **`src/midi.js`:** the "scroll to the controller" in MIDI setup opens the drawer instead. Profile actions open the side panel's Controller tab.
- **`src/boot.js`:** calls `frameApply()` at start and on changes to `FR_MQ`.
- **No new assistant actions,** no sound changes and no Worker change.
- **Tests:**
  - A smoke line: at 1440×900 the page has no vertical scroll, `#tracks` and `#asIn` are visible, the drawer cycles through its modes, and the editor opens docked.
  - qa-tester adds a `tests/frame.mjs`: 1440×900 and 1366×768 in all three themes, plus `tests/phone.mjs` still passes at 390 and 360 px.
- **Dependency:** UX-4 (phone layout) is still waiting on its test-link OK and isn't on main. Step 1 should be built after UX-4 ships, or on top of `preview-ux-4`, so the two node-moving layouts never fight. Each checks that the other is off.

## 5. Effort and risks
**Effort:** step 1 is L (about the size of UX-4, but with no new features). Steps 2-6 are M, S, M, M, M.

**Risks:**
- **Moving nodes:** some code may assume the old page order, such as scrolling to a section or measuring from the page top. I found three places (`openEditor`, MIDI setup, the playhead). Moving the nodes back when the frame is off keeps the tablet page as it is today. Mitigation: a full smoke run plus the new frame test in all three themes.
- **Short laptops:** a 1366×768 screen gives the browser about 650 px of height. Below a 760 px viewport the drawer starts hidden (Keyboard is one click away). Below 600 px the frame switches off and the page behaves as today.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** the new shortcuts all use Ctrl/Cmd+Alt, which the computer-keyboard piano already ignores. On some European keyboards Ctrl+Alt acts as AltGr; we only act when no text field has focus.
- **The controller stays the brand:** it looks the same, keeps its keys and knobs, and is one click from Full. On first visit it opens as Keyboard (decision 2).
- **Phones and tablets are untouched,** and all the editor features listed above keep working (checked by the smoke test).

## 6. Decisions for the owner
1. **Which step first?**
   - (a) The one-screen frame **(recommended)**.
   - (b) Dense track lanes first. It's smaller and very visible, but it lives on today's long page.
   - (c) The control bar first.
2. **How the controller drawer starts.**
   - (a) **Keyboard row open; your choice is remembered (recommended).** It's hidden on short laptop screens.
   - (b) Closed, showing only the header row.
   - (c) The full controller.
3. **Where Keys sits.**
   - (a) **Right sidebar, 320 px, foldable to an orb rail (recommended).** The chat needs height, and the bottom of the screen already belongs to the editor and the controller.
   - (b) A bottom strip above the controller.
   - (c) A floating panel over the tracks.
